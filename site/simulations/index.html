Simulations

Two job simulations designed by the banks themselves and run on Forage — one in investment banking, one in wealth management.

Investment Banking

Bank of America · Mergers & acquisitions · Sep 2026

As an analyst on the Technology, Media & Telecom team, I advised Webflix, a U.S. streaming service, on finding a company to acquire.

  1. Ran a SWOT analysis and built a company profile from a 10-K.
  2. Screened a shortlist of targets against the client’s strategic criteria and picked the best fit.
  3. Built a DCF model to calculate the target’s implied equity value and share price.
  4. Ran a sensitivity analysis on the key assumptions and presented the findings.

SWOT · 10-K analysis · M&A screening · DCF · Sensitivity analysis · Excel

Wealth Management

Citi Wealth · Portfolio construction & client advisory · Sep 2026

As a wealth analyst, I was assigned a client who had just sold the family business for $100 million and needed a plan for what came next.

  1. Ran client discovery to understand the client’s finances, goals, and preferences.
  2. Built a portfolio proposal in Excel using asset allocation and security selection, aimed at moderate risk and tax efficiency.
  3. Presented the strategy to the client in plain language.

Asset allocation · Security selection · Tax efficiency · Risk management · Client communication

Both were completed on Forage, where companies publish self-paced simulations of their teams’ real work. They’re training programmes, not employment with Bank of America or Citi.
