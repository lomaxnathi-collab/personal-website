Simulations

Two hands-on simulations built by the banks themselves and run on Forage: valuing an acquisition as an investment banking analyst at Bank of America, and advising a newly wealthy client as a wealth analyst at Citi.

Investment banking · Sep 2026

Bank of America — Investment Banking

Mergers & acquisitions · Technology, Media & Telecom · via Forage

The scenario

Working as an analyst on Bank of America’s Technology, Media & Telecom coverage team, advising Webflix — a U.S. streaming subscription service — as it looks for a company to acquire.

What I did

  1. Profiled the market. Ran a SWOT analysis and pulled key figures from a company’s 10-K into a concise company profile.
  2. Picked the target. Screened a shortlist of potential acquisitions against the client’s strategic criteria and identified the strongest fit.
  3. Valued it. Built a discounted cash flow (DCF) model to calculate the target’s implied equity value and share price.
  4. Stress-tested it. Ran a sensitivity analysis to show how the valuation moves as key assumptions, like discount rate and growth, change — and presented the findings.

Skills

  • SWOT analysis
  • 10-K analysis
  • M&A screening
  • DCF valuation
  • Sensitivity analysis
  • Excel

Wealth management · Sep 2026

Citi Wealth

Portfolio construction & client advisory · via Forage

The scenario

Working as a wealth analyst at Citi, assigned to a client who has just sold the family business for $100 million and needs a plan for what comes next.

What I did

  1. Ran client discovery. Asked the questions that uncover a client’s financial situation, goals, and investment preferences.
  2. Built the portfolio. Constructed a proposal in Excel using asset allocation and security selection, designed for moderate risk tolerance and tax efficiency.
  3. Made the case. Created and delivered a client presentation explaining the strategy in plain language — turning complex investment concepts into something the client could trust.

Skills

  • Asset allocation
  • Security selection
  • Tax efficiency
  • Risk management
  • Client communication
  • Presentation

Both simulations were completed on Forage, where companies publish self-paced job simulations that mirror the real work of their teams. They are training programmes, not employment with Bank of America or Citi.
